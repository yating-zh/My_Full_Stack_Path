Understand of
    __filename
    __dirname
    path.dirname()
    path.basename()
    path.extname()
    path.join()
    path.resolve()
    path.normalize()

For example, __filename is: /users/example/item/image.png
    path.dirname(__filename)               /users/example/item
    path.basename(__filename)              image.png
    path.extname(__filename)               .png
    path.join("seg1","seg2")               seg1/seg2
    path.join("/seg1","seg2")              /seg1/seg2
    path.resolve("seg1","seg2")            /current_DIR/seg1/seg2
    path.resolve("/seg1","seg2")           /seg1/seg2
    path.resolve("/seg1","/seg2")          /seg2
    path.normalize("/seg1/.seg2/../seg3")  /seg1/seg3
